# v5 results — grounding, answerability, and one test pass

**Evaluation unit:** FEVER claims (999 validation, 1,998 test).
**Test pass:** spent once after freezing models, features, thresholds, and hashes.
**Serving:** learned path offline; fixed 0.7/0.95 rule served.

## Protocol

- Fit: 9,000 balanced FEVER train claims.
- Select: 999 validation claims.
- Feature block: 30 v4 features + 15 MiniCheck-Flan-T5-Large grounding features.
- Verdict: standardised multinomial logistic regression.
- Answerability: HistGradientBoosting with base features, verdict probabilities,
  and grounding features.
- Policy: robust validation rule at `tau=0.62`, requiring verifiable coverage
  >= 0.81 and overall coverage >= 0.65 while minimising NEI false answers.
- Test: 1,998 claims, one final pass.

## What the result means

The policy answered **1,324 / 1,998** claims at **78.70%** overall precision and
**1,089 / 1,332** verifiable claims at **95.50%** precision. It abstained on
**64.71%** of NEI claims; the **35.29%** it answered were wrong **99.15%** of the
time.

If abstentions are scored as wrong, effective binary accuracy on verifiable claims
is `0.81757 * 0.95500 = 0.7808`.

The 95.5% number is selective precision on the answered subset, not accuracy on all
verifiable claims. NEI is reduced, not solved. Overall precision is still 78.7%,
below a 90% serving bar.
