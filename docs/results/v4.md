# v4 results — reranker and data expansion

**Evaluation unit:** FEVER validation claims (999).
**Test pass:** **not spent**. v4 stopped before P5 because the NEI gate was
unreachable on validation.
**Serving:** learned detector offline; fixed rule served.

## Reranker + refit

| Arm | Val macro-F1 | INSUFFICIENT F1 | Kept |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| v1 hybrid | 0.67636 | 0.52073 | baseline |
| Rerank top-5 | **0.69296** | **0.53559** | **yes** |
| Rerank top-8 | 0.67732 | 0.51581 | no |

Top-5 is a **+0.0166** validation macro-F1 improvement and **+0.0149**
INSUFFICIENT F1. Top-8 diluted the gain.

## Training-data expansion

| Train size | Base val macro-F1 | Answerability AUROC | Best NEI | Overall precision |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 3,000 | 0.69296 | — | — | — |
| 6,000 | 0.69061 | 0.781 | 0.453 | 0.749 |
| 9,000 | 0.68515 | 0.787 | 0.423 | 0.761 |

More data improved NEI separation but lowered base macro-F1. The two goals pulled
in opposite directions.

## 9k answerability tuning

A cost-sensitive HistGradientBoosting model with NEI weight 3 reached:

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Answerability AUROC | **0.794** |
| NEI coverage | **0.408** |
| Verifiable coverage | 0.800 |
| Overall precision | 0.768 |

That was still **0.008 above** the 0.40 NEI gate. A joint search over `tau` and
class thresholds found **no feasible point**; the best NEI under the verifiable
constraints was **0.417** at verifiable coverage **0.809**.

## Interpretation

- Reranking and more data were real improvements, but not enough for promotion.
- Macro-F1 and NEI recognition were not aligned at 9k.
- No test predictions exist, and the learned model remained offline.
